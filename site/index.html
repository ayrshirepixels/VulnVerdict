The problem

Everything arrives labelled "critical". You have forty other jobs.

A 9.9 that needs someone at the keyboard with a USB stick is not a priority. A 9.1 that anyone on the internet can trigger against your VPN is. Scanners, vendor mails and the news score both the same way, because a score describes the bug, not your estate.

VulnVerdict is the layer above your scanner and your patch tool. It matches every public vulnerability against what you actually run, works out how an attacker would have to get at it from where it sits, and gives you a verdict you can repeat to your boss.

Why open source

It holds a map of your weakest points. You should be able to read it.

Every line of the console, the connectors and the decision table is public under the AGPL-3.0. Run it for free in a container or on a VM in your network, forever, with no asset cap and no registration. Audit what leaves the box (nothing about your estate does). Fix the adapter for the odd switch nobody else has and send it back.

We make our living from support subscriptions and a curated feed, not from locking the door. That keeps us honest about the product, and it means the tool a fifty-person firm relies on cannot be pulled out from under them.

  • Free tier: the whole console, every connector, every public feed, community support on GitHub. No cap.
  • Supported: the signed curated feed with pre-written plain-English explanations, a tested update channel, and email support with a response time.
  • MSP: everything above per client, plus the portal that shows every client's exposure in one view.
Support plans
What you get

One short email. That is the product.

What to fix today, what to fix this week, what changed since last time, and nothing else. If the email is good enough you never open the console. We count that as success.

A reason with every verdict

"FortiOS 7.2.5 on FW-EDGE-01: exploited in the wild, works over the network with no login, reachable from the internet. Fixed in 7.2.8." One sentence a non-specialist can repeat.

Evidence you can check

Every verdict keeps its chain: which feed said what, when it was retrieved, which rule fired. Including the ones dismissed as not affected, because a false negative is the dangerous direction.

Nothing to tune

Scoring, thresholds, digest timing and suppression rules ship configured. The only setup questions are what you run and what faces the internet, and the connectors answer most of that themselves.

Your estate stays yours

Runs in a container or on a VM in your network. Feeds come in; your inventory never goes out. AI explanations are optional and BYOM, bring your own model: your Anthropic or OpenAI key, or a local model that never leaves the building. Hostnames and addresses are stripped first.

Works with what you have

Firewalls from Fortinet, Palo Alto, SonicWall, Sophos, WatchGuard, Meraki, Check Point, Zyxel and UniFi, plus pfSense and OPNsense. Microsoft, VMware, Linux, printers, switches, out-of-band controllers, SBOMs from your build pipeline. Tickets into Jira, ServiceNow, Freshservice, Zendesk, Azure DevOps, HaloPSA, Autotask, or plain email.

Nothing to babysit

Feeds update themselves, connectors report when they lose access, and the console emails you if it stops working. Silence means nothing to do, never that the box died.

Install

Fifteen minutes, three ways. All free.

Containerised or a VM: whichever your estate already runs. The console, the verdicts and the updates are the same either way, and only HTTPS needs to be reachable.

One container

Database, console and TLS inside a single image. One docker run, one volume, on any Docker host you already have.

Docker Compose

Postgres, worker, console and Caddy as separate services for people who already run Docker.

Virtual machine (OVA)

A ready-built appliance for VMware or VirtualBox, or Hyper-V after a one-line disk conversion. Import it, answer three questions on the console, open the URL. No Docker knowledge needed.

docker run -d --name vulnverdict --restart unless-stopped \
  -p 443:443 -p 80:80 -v vulnverdict:/data -e VV_HOSTNAME=vulnverdict.internal \
  ghcr.io/ayrshirepixels/vulnverdict:allinone
  1. Start it. Run the container or import the OVA. Create the administrator account on the first page.
  2. Say where mail goes. SMTP, SendGrid or Brevo, and who receives the digest.
  3. Add what you run. Paste read-only credentials for the connectors you have, or type the products into the watchlist. The first digest arrives the next morning.

What it is not
  • Not a scanner. It reads your scanner's output; it never probes for vulnerabilities itself.
  • Not a patch tool. It says what to fix. Your RMM or your hands do the fixing.
  • Not a SIEM or a helpdesk. It emits to those.
  • Not scored by an AI. Deterministic rules decide the verdict. The AI, if you turn it on, explains it.
Built for
  • The IT manager or sole sysadmin at a 50 to 500 seat organisation, with no security team.
  • Managed service providers who need every client's exposure in one view.
  • Cyber Essentials holders who must patch criticals and highs within 14 days and want to show they did.
  • Anyone whose boss asks "are we affected by the thing on the news".
