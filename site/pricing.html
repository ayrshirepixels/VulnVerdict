Questions

Straight answers.

Is the free version crippled?

No. Same code, same connectors, same verdicts, no asset cap. The console pulls the public feeds itself instead of our curated bundle, and support is the GitHub issue tracker. Plenty of small firms will never need more.

So what am I paying for?

The curated feed (built once for everyone, signed, with plain-English explanations already written), tested updates you can roll back with one command, and a human who answers email. The things a one-person team should not have to do at seven in the morning.

How much is a support plan?

From £100 a month, billed annually. The price depends on the size of your estate and how much help you want, so we agree it after a short call rather than make you pick a band from a table.

Does my inventory leave my network?

No, on any plan. The console runs in your network, in a container or on a VM. It pulls feeds in; nothing about your assets goes out. MSP reporting, if a client turns it on, sends counts and verdict sentences with asset names stripped.

Which licence?

AGPL-3.0. Run it, change it, redistribute it. If you offer it as a service to others you publish your changes. The VulnVerdict name and logo are trademarks, so a fork needs its own name. Contributions are welcome under the same licence.

What are you liable for if a verdict is wrong?

Nothing beyond what the support agreement says, and the software carries no warranty. A verdict is advice worked out from public data and what your connectors report; no tool can fully know your environment, which is why every verdict shows its evidence for a person to check. Patching decisions, and their consequences, stay with the people who run the systems. The full advisory notice says this properly.

Is it a scanner?

No. It reads your scanner's findings as a second opinion and matches vendor data against what your connectors report. The only network activity of its own is an optional host discovery sweep, which never runs vulnerability checks.

Does it need an internet connection?

Outbound HTTPS to the feeds, or only to our bundle service on a support plan. Fully air-gapped sites upload a signed bundle by hand; the console refuses anything unsigned or older than what it already has.

What is BYOM?

Bring your own model. The AI explanations are optional on every plan, and you choose the model: paste your own Anthropic or OpenAI key, or point the console at a local model such as one running in Ollama, so nothing leaves the building. We never resell AI usage or see your prompts. The model explains a verdict in plain English and describes how an attacker would go about it. It never decides a verdict, and it never sees a hostname or an address.

Does it run in a container or on a VM?

Either. Run the single container on any Docker host with one command and one volume, use Docker Compose if you already run Docker, or import the ready-built VM appliance (OVA) into VMware or VirtualBox, or into Hyper-V after a one-line disk conversion. Two virtual CPUs, 4 GB of memory and 40 GB of disk is plenty, and only HTTPS needs to be reachable (port 80 just redirects to it).
