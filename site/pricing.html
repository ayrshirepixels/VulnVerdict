Questions

Straight answers.

Does my inventory leave my network?

No. The console runs on your VM. It pulls feeds in; nothing about your assets goes out. MSP reporting, if you turn it on, sends counts and verdict sentences with asset names stripped.

Is it a scanner?

No. It reads your scanner's findings as a second opinion and matches vendor data against what your connectors report. The only network activity of its own is an optional host discovery sweep, which never runs vulnerability checks.

Does it need an internet connection?

Outbound HTTPS to the feeds, or only to our central bundle service. Fully air-gapped sites upload a signed bundle by hand; the console refuses anything unsigned or older than what it already has.

What about the AI bit?

Optional. Bring your own Anthropic or OpenAI key, or point it at a local model. It explains a verdict in plain English and describes how an attacker would go about it. It never decides a verdict, and it never sees a hostname or an address.

Can I try it without paying?

Yes. The console works without a licence against the public feeds, with no asset cap. A licence adds the signed bundle, the pre-written explanations and support. Ask for a trial key and we will send one the same day.

What if we outgrow a tier?

Collection pauses at the cap, nothing is deleted, and the console tells you. Move up a tier or archive what you no longer run.
